Our story

Pixel Designs started as the media-buying desk of Nora Group of Companies, built to help brands show up consistently wherever their audience already is. What began as a small team booking a handful of formats has grown into a full advertising practice spanning television, digital video, websites, public transport and malls — all planned and bought as one connected campaign rather than five separate bookings.

About the founder

Pixel Designs is led by Ammus, a BCA graduate based in Irinjalakuda, Kerala. Ammus's background is in advertising outreach — building and managing contact databases and supermarket advertising collaborations, including stand and decor placements for FMCG brands in the Jeddah region of Saudi Arabia. That hands-on experience with retail and regional media placement shaped Pixel Designs' approach: plan the campaign once, then place it everywhere it needs to be seen.
